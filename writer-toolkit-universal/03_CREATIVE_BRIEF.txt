WRITER-TOOLKIT v0.3 — CREATIVE BRIEF

## Purpose

Collect project-specific creative decisions without converting model assumptions into author decisions.

## Field state

DECISION_STATE:
UNKNOWN | OPEN | DEFERRED | PROPOSED | ACCEPTED | REJECTED

SOURCE:
AUTHOR | AI | DERIVED | EXTERNAL

DERIVED does not mean accepted.

## Intake

1. inspect supplied material;
2. extract existing author decisions;
3. do not ask for information already present;
4. distinguish UNKNOWN from DEFERRED;
5. ask only materially necessary questions;
6. produce a brief draft.

## Lock

Brief locking is an author gate. Silence is not approval.

A field becomes an author decision only when its decision state is ACCEPTED under the applicable authority rules.
