===== SER.01_SERIES_ARCHITECTURE.md =====
# SER.01 — Series Architecture

## Purpose

Model the relationship between the current book and any approved larger arc without allowing future plans to distort the current manuscript by default.

## Required fields

```text
CURRENT_BOOK_PROMISE
CURRENT_BOOK_SCOPE
BOOK_RESOLUTION
SERIES_ARC_STATUS
FUTURE_SEEDS
RESERVED_REVEALS
ESCALATION_PLAN
UNUSED_OPTIONALITY
```

## Rule

A future seed is a design reserve, not an accepted event.

A future reveal is not current canon unless explicitly accepted as such.

===== SER.02_BOOK_LOCK.md =====
# SER.02 — Book Lock

## Purpose

Protect the current book from uncontrolled series expansion during production.

## Book Lock records

```text
LOCKED_BOOK_SCOPE
LOCKED_END_STATE
LOCKED_LOAD_BEARING_ARCS
RESERVED_SERIES_SPACE
OPEN_SERIES_OPTIONS
DEFERRED_SERIES_DECISIONS
```

## Gate

When the current-book lock is active, a series idea that changes a load-bearing current-book element becomes a development proposal and requires the appropriate author decision.

Silence is not approval.

===== SER.03_SERIES_CONTINUITY.md =====
# SER.03 — Series Continuity

## Purpose

Track continuity across books after each book is locked or completed.

## Checks

- dates and chronology;
- character state;
- knowledge state;
- world-rule consistency;
- reserved reveals;
- unresolved promises;
- deliberate contradictions / deviations.

## Authority rule

Series digest is a navigation aid. Exact contradictions are resolved against the manuscript and authoritative project state, not by trusting summary prose.

## Audit outcome

Continuity findings may be:

```text
MATCH
CONFLICT
UNVERIFIED
INTENTIONAL_DEVIATION
OPEN
```

===== SERIES_MODULE.md =====
# Series Module

## Module ID

`COND.10_SERIES`

## Activation

```text
SERIES = ON
```

or another explicit series intent / multi-book plan / episodic architecture flag.

## Fields

```text
BOOK_STATE
SERIES_ARC
RESERVED_REVEALS
ESCALATION
LONG_TERM_CONTINUITY
```

## Scope rule

The current book has its own author-approved scope. Future-book possibilities are tracked separately and do not become current-book canon automatically.

## Inactive behavior

Standalone projects do not inherit series-specific obligations, hooks, seeds, cliffhangers, or continuity machinery merely because they may later become expandable.
