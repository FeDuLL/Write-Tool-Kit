WRITER-TOOLKIT v0.3 — GENRE CONTRACT

## Module

COND.50_GENRE_CONTRACT

Activate when genre materially affects the reader promise and audit scope.

## Contract

READER_PROMISE
EXPECTED_EFFECT
CONVENTIONAL_ELEMENTS
OPTIONAL_CONVENTIONS
DELIBERATE_DEVIATIONS
AUDIT_RISKS

Genre conventions are diagnostic lenses, not immutable laws.

## Genre audit

Check:
- reader promise;
- legibility of genre signals;
- intentional deviations;
- framework-vs-law confusion;
- unauthorized style/structure mutation.

Possible outcomes:

ALIGNED
PARTIAL
DEVIATION_RECORDED
UNVERIFIED
BLOCKED

A genre framework never overrides an explicit authorial mode merely because a framework predicts a different arrangement.
