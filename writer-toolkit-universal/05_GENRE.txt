# Genre Contract Module

## Module ID

`COND.50_GENRE_CONTRACT`

## Activation

Activate when the author requests genre contracting or when genre materially affects the reader promise and audit scope.

## Contract fields

```text
READER_PROMISE
EXPECTED_EFFECT
CONVENTIONAL_ELEMENTS
OPTIONAL_CONVENTIONS
DELIBERATE_DEVIATIONS
AUDIT_RISKS
```

## Principle

Genre conventions are diagnostic lenses, not immutable laws.

Structural frameworks and genre models may inform diagnosis, but they never override an explicit authorial mode merely because a framework predicts a different arrangement.

## Rule

===== GNR.02_GENRE_CONTRACT.md =====
# GNR.02 — Genre Contract

## Build the contract

1. Define the reader promise.
2. Identify conventional signals that support that promise.
3. Mark conventions that are optional.
4. Record deliberate deviations explicitly when load-bearing.
5. Convert the resulting contract into project-state fields.

## Deviation semantics

```text
PROPOSED
ACCEPTED
REJECTED
DEFERRED
```

`DEFERRED` remains unresolved and cannot silently become `ACCEPTED`.

## Author sovereignty

An intentional artistic deviation can be valid even when it conflicts with a conventional genre expectation, provided the project can still articulate what reader experience it is promising.

===== GNR.03_GENRE_AUDIT.md =====
# GNR.03 — Genre Audit

## Audit questions

- Does the work fulfill its declared reader promise?
- Are genre signals sufficiently legible for the declared audience?
- Are deviations intentional and represented in state?
- Is a framework being mistaken for a law?
- Are genre expectations causing unauthorized style or structure mutations?

## Outcomes

```text
ALIGNED
PARTIAL
DEVIATION_RECORDED
UNVERIFIED
BLOCKED
```

The audit is descriptive and corrective. It does not rank genres or impose a universal formula.
