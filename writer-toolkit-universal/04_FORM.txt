WRITER-TOOLKIT v0.3 — FORM

## Form Contract

FORM is an operational routing layer, not a genre rule.

Every project resolves:
- VOLUME_METRIC
- STRUCTURAL_UNIT
- PRODUCTION_UNIT
- LOCK_UNIT
- DONE_CRITERIA
- AUDIT_PROFILE
- PERSISTENCE_PROFILE

The runtime does not choose the literary form.

### Prose

Possible units include CHAPTER, SCENE, SEQUENCE, EPISODE or another project-defined unit.

### Screenplay

STRUCTURAL_UNIT = SCENE / SEQUENCE
PRODUCTION_UNIT = SCENE / SEQUENCE
CHAPTER_MODEL = OFF
VOLUME_METRIC = project-defined

## Complexity

T1 = COMPACT
T2 = STANDARD
T3 = HIGH-COMPLEXITY

Complexity controls process depth/resources only. It does not activate hidden world, mystery, series, IP or commercial modules.

## Screenplay routing

Never import prose chapter logic into screenplay production or audit.

Output budget may cause chunking but may not change the selected structural unit.
