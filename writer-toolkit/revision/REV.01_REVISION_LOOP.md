# REV.01_REVISION_LOOP

**ID:** `REV.01_REVISION_LOOP`

## DIAGNOSE

Identify the failed audit/check or explicit author-requested problem.

## LOCATE

Identify the affected scene(s), sequence(s), chapter(s), unit(s), or arc(s).

## PROPOSE

Select the smallest effective change.

Include trade-offs and author-voice risk where material.

## TEST

Check that the proposed change preserves:

- character voice;
- causality;
- continuity;
- accepted author decisions;
- Style Lock / intentional local deviation rules.

## REWRITE

Apply only the authorized change.

## RE-AUDIT

Run the relevant checks again.

## RECORD

Update:

- change log;
- relevant state;
- deviation record where applicable;
- audit coverage.

## FIREWALL

Revision does not provide authority to change premise, canon, ending, form,
major scope, or other author-only decisions.

Those are escalated through Core authority.
