# DEV.06_COINCIDENCE_AUDIT

**ID:** `DEV.06_COINCIDENCE_AUDIT`

## COINCIDENCE MAY INTRODUCE

- situation;
- encounter;
- information.

## COINCIDENCE SHOULD NOT

Silently resolve the central conflict unless the effect is intentionally designed
and supported by the work.

## AUDIT QUESTION

Does coincidence perform load-bearing causal work that should instead be supported
by established causality, character choice/action, or deliberate design?

## OUTPUT

Finding + evidence + proposed action where applicable.

The audit does not edit the manuscript.
