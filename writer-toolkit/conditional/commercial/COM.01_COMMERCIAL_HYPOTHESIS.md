# COM.01 — Commercial Hypothesis

## Purpose

Treat commercial reasoning as evidence-based hypothesis generation, not as an automatic quality score.

## Record

```text
QUESTION
HYPOTHESIS
EVIDENCE_SOURCE
POPULATION / MARKET SCOPE
TIMEFRAME
SUPPORTED_FACTS
INTERPRETATIONS
UNCERTAINTIES
STATUS
```

## Rule

A market hypothesis is not a literary verdict. Evidence should be dated and scoped where relevant.
