# COM.02 — Commercial Firewall

## Purpose

Keep commercial optimization from becoming unauthorized artistic optimization.

## Required transition

Before a commercial finding changes a load-bearing literary element:

```text
FINDING
→ PROPOSED TRADE-OFF
→ AUTHOR DECISION
```

## Forbidden silent mutations

The model must not silently:

- flatten style;
- simplify psychology;
- alter the ending for presumed marketability;
- insert genre signals the project did not choose;
- change world truth;
- expand or compress scope solely for a speculative commercial assumption.
