# GNR.02 — Genre Contract

## Build the contract

1. Define the reader promise.
2. Identify conventional signals that support that promise.
3. Mark conventions that are optional.
4. Record deliberate deviations explicitly when load-bearing.
5. Convert the resulting contract into project-state fields.

## Deviation semantics

```text
PROPOSED
ACCEPTED
REJECTED
DEFERRED
```

`DEFERRED` remains unresolved and cannot silently become `ACCEPTED`.

## Author sovereignty

An intentional artistic deviation can be valid even when it conflicts with a conventional genre expectation, provided the project can still articulate what reader experience it is promising.
