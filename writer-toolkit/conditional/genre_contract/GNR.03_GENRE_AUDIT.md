# GNR.03 — Genre Audit

## Audit questions

- Does the work fulfill its declared reader promise?
- Are genre signals sufficiently legible for the declared audience?
- Are deviations intentional and represented in state?
- Is a framework being mistaken for a law?
- Are genre expectations causing unauthorized style or structure mutations?

## Outcomes

```text
ALIGNED
PARTIAL
DEVIATION_RECORDED
UNVERIFIED
BLOCKED
```

The audit is descriptive and corrective. It does not rank genres or impose a universal formula.
