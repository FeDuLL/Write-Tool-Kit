# Series Module

## Module ID

`COND.10_SERIES`

## Activation

```text
SERIES = ON
```

or another explicit series intent / multi-book plan / episodic architecture flag.

## Fields

```text
BOOK_STATE
SERIES_ARC
RESERVED_REVEALS
ESCALATION
LONG_TERM_CONTINUITY
```

## Scope rule

The current book has its own author-approved scope. Future-book possibilities are tracked separately and do not become current-book canon automatically.

## Inactive behavior

Standalone projects do not inherit series-specific obligations, hooks, seeds, cliffhangers, or continuity machinery merely because they may later become expandable.
