# HW.04 — Reveal Audits

## Audit classes

### Leak Audit
Checks for accidental direct or indirect disclosure before the intended point.

### Fairness Audit
Checks whether the reveal has sufficient information support for the promised reader experience.

### Retrospective Coherence Audit
Checks whether the revealed truth makes prior evidence more coherent without requiring impossible knowledge.

### Epistemic Integrity Audit
Checks character knowledge, narrator access, and reader access separately.

## Read-only rule

Audits may report findings and propose repairs. They do not silently rewrite manuscript, state, canon, or reveal schedule.
